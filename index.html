About

Full-stack by nature. Creative by curiosity.

I’m Muhammad Shees, a Full-Stack Developer based in Karachi, Pakistan. I build applications from the interface down to the data layer.

My work spans React frontends, Node.js APIs, and MongoDB and SQL databases. I completed an Advanced Diploma in Software Engineering at Aptech and am currently studying for a BS in Software Engineering at the Virtual University of Pakistan.

I’m especially interested in the creative end of frontend development — interaction, motion and real-time 3D. I also use AI coding agents with a written plan, review every change, and make the engineering calls myself.
